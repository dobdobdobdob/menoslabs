A lift in customer experience

Our client — an established event & touring management platform — came to us for a lift in their customer experience: a dated interface their loyal users had outgrown. Menos Labs' approach: evolve the product's own identity instead of replacing it. We kept the look, navigation, and muscle memory their veterans rely on, and reworked the screen to surface what actually drives their day — budget, schedule, people, and communication.
