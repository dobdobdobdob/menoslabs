About Menos Labs

AI should serve people —
not the other way around.

Menos Labs is an ethical AI consultancy for leaders navigating the most consequential technology shift of their careers. We advise on AI strategy, lead transformation programs, accelerate start-up growth, and craft brand strategies that hold up in a world where AI is reshaping every category.

Our practice is grounded in one conviction: AI should serve people, not the other way around. Every engagement begins with a hard question about what's right — then we do the work to make it real.

We're builders first, consultants second. That's why our clients get working systems, not slide decks — and why they trust us with the decisions that actually matter.

01 — Ethics First
Transparent by design
AI should augment human judgment, not replace it. We build systems transparent enough to trust and robust enough to scale — without trading integrity for speed.
02 — Operator Mindset
We've sat in your seat
Fortune 500 retail, PE-backed start-ups, scrappy agencies — we know the difference between a deck and a decision because we've made the decisions ourselves.
03 — Outcomes, Not Theater
Measured by what moves
Revenue, adoption, retention, margin — not hours billed or slide counts. Every engagement is wired to the metric that matters most to you.

01 / AI
AI Strategy

From opportunity mapping to deployed systems. We help you identify where AI creates real leverage, then build or integrate the tools to capture it — with governance, ethics, and measurable outcomes baked in from day one.

02 / Transformation
Transformation

Operating model redesign, workflow automation, and change management for companies adapting to the AI era. We lead the hard work of turning strategy into new ways of working — not just new slide decks.

03 / Growth
Start-up Growth

Product-market fit, go-to-market execution, and scaling discipline for founders at the critical growth stage. Fractional CPO and CRO support from operators who've been through it — not just read about it.

Brand Strategy

Positioning, narrative, and identity for companies building category-defining brands. We help you stand for something clear in a market that's increasingly noisy and AI-saturated.
